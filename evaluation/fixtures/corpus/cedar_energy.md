# Cedar Campus — Energy Retrofit

Cedar Campus is a fictional university energy demonstrator. Rooftop solar panels feed a battery storage controller and a heat-pump system serving three teaching buildings. Engineers compare predicted electricity production with meter readings every fifteen minutes. A dashboard alerts operators when the battery state of charge falls below 18 percent.

The energy team piloted thermal storage using a water tank charged overnight. Its primary objective was to reduce peak grid imports during winter mornings, not to replace the campus emergency generator. A monthly report separates photovoltaic production, grid purchases and heating demand.

The laboratory also studies cell defects using camera images, but those experiments are separate from operational power metering. Technicians use inverter logs to investigate unexpected shutdowns and archive all firmware changes. The campus does not own the wind turbines maintained by the coastal harbor authority.
