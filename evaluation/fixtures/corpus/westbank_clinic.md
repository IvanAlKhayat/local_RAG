# Westbank Clinic — Appointment and Laboratory Workflow

Westbank is a fictional outpatient clinic. The reception team schedules routine blood tests, while a qualified nurse reviews urgent referrals. Laboratory samples are labeled with a barcode and matched to an appointment record before transport. The clinic's standard processing window for routine results is two working days.

Only authorized medical staff can view named patient results. An audit trail records access to the electronic health record; public performance reports contain aggregated figures without identifiers. Technicians validate the barcode scanners every Monday morning.

The clinic's inventory system tracks reagent expiration dates and requests replacement stock when quantities fall below a configured threshold. This is a healthcare workflow, distinct from the warehouse robot inventory experiment and from the library's document archive.
