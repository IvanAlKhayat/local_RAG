# Pinewatch Forest — Wildfire Observation

Pinewatch is a fictional forest monitoring initiative that combines satellite observations with ground reports. Analysts inspect Sentinel-2 imagery and its shortwave infrared bands for evidence of recent burns. Optical satellite scenes with heavy cloud cover are flagged as unsuitable for immediate classification. Field teams send geotagged observations to verify suspected fire scars.

The operations center produces a hazard map every Thursday. A machine-learning classifier groups the imagery into vegetation, soil, water and burned areas. Results are reviewed by an analyst before publication because shadowed slopes can resemble charred ground.

Pinewatch's monitoring network uses a solar-powered field gateway, but it is not the Cedar Campus energy retrofit. During an active fire, the response team relies on emergency services; the remote-sensing program supplies information and does not autonomously dispatch firefighting crews.
