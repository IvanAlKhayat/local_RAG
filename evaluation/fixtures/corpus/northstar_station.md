# Northstar Research Station — Winter Operations

Northstar is a fictional polar research outpost. Its winter crew operates a blue-light lidar for aerosol observations, a compact weather mast for wind measurements, and backup lithium battery packs for emergency communications. The observation team publishes a daily weather digest at 06:40 local time. Sensor files are checked before publication by the duty meteorologist.

When the anemometer is iced over, technicians cross-check wind estimates against the lidar and the coastal beacon. Storm alerts are issued when sustained winds exceed 24 meters per second. The emergency protocol requires a satellite-radio check every eight hours, even when the terrestrial data link appears healthy. The station stores instrument calibrations in an offline ledger.

The station's field manual distinguishes between science observations and maintenance activities. Calibration certificates remain in the technical archive; public digests contain only quality-controlled measurements. A separate observatory project, not Northstar, schedules visits from school classes.
