# Silver River — Water Quality Monitoring

Silver River is a fictional freshwater monitoring project. Automated probes measure turbidity, dissolved oxygen and nitrate concentration at four sampling stations. The laboratory checks nitrate against a local study threshold of 12 milligrams per liter. Samples from the upstream station are collected every Tuesday at 09:30.

During a flood, the field team may suspend manual sampling for safety. The sensor gateway transmits measurements over a low-power radio link and caches readings whenever mobile coverage is unavailable. A calibration log records replacement of dissolved-oxygen probes.

The program does not operate ferry crossings or manage harbor tides; Cobalt Harbor handles those tasks. Silver River reports water-quality trends to the municipal environmental office, which reviews anomalous readings before public release.
