# Alder Museum — Collection Conservation

Alder Museum is a fictional cultural institution that conserves paintings and parchment manuscripts. Conservators limit ultraviolet exposure in the exhibition hall and record relative humidity in display cases. The target humidity band for the parchment gallery is 45 to 55 percent. The museum closes the conservation laboratory to visitors on Mondays.

New acquisitions receive an accession number, a condition report and a high-resolution reference photograph. The collection team uses cotton gloves for selected handling tasks and documents all restoration materials. Fragile manuscripts are displayed for short rotations to reduce light damage.

Although Elmbridge Library also handles historical documents, the museum does not perform the library's newspaper OCR processing. The museum's environmental dashboard supports preventive conservation, not weather forecasting or energy-grid control.
