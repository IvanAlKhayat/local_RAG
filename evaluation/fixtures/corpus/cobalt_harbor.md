# Cobalt Harbor — Ferry Safety Plan

Cobalt Harbor is a fictional coastal transport hub operating passenger ferries and service boats. Harbor pilots monitor Automatic Identification System (AIS) transmissions, tidal height and visibility before authorizing departures. A yellow warning beacon is illuminated when fog reduces visibility below 400 meters. Each morning a dispatcher records the first departure at 07:15.

Maintenance engineers inspect pier joints for saltwater corrosion and check the emergency gangway before the commuter ferry arrives. Inspection records are kept in a digital logbook for five years. An ultrasonic sensor on the eastern quay measures the water level, while a separate river-monitoring project measures nitrate concentration upstream.

Passengers with reduced mobility may request a portable boarding ramp from the terminal desk. This harbor ramp is not the low-floor train boarding system described by the Meridian Rail program. During severe storms, the pilot can suspend all crossings regardless of the published timetable.
