# Meridian Rail — Accessible Passenger Service

Meridian Rail is a fictional regional passenger service. Its accessibility program deploys portable platform ramps at stations that lack level boarding. Travelers are asked to request assistance 24 hours in advance, although unplanned help may be available depending on staffing. The central dispatch office monitors service interruptions and informs passengers through station displays.

Maintenance crews inspect door sensors and wheelchair spaces during overnight train checks. An onboard counter estimates carriage occupancy but does not record passenger identity. The timetable includes a 12-minute interchange at the central junction for the afternoon regional connection.

The rail program shares the concept of boarding ramps with Cobalt Harbor's ferry service, but it uses different equipment and operating procedures. Station announcements are available in two languages. Engineering staff keep inspection logs for traceability.
